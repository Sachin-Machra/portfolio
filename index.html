From fintech APIs to real-time AI

I'm a Python engineer who started in fintech — building Django REST services for an NBFC handling eNACH mandates, UPI Autopay, and bank integrations with end-to-end AES-GCM/RSA encryption and RBI-compliant workflows.

Then I went deep into AI & computer vision: I built a smart surveillance platform that fine-tunes YOLO11 on custom-labelled data (trained on NVIDIA T4 GPUs), detects dangerous activity near farm sites, performs ANPR with PaddleOCR + SAHI tiled inference, and runs a face-recognition access-control system with InsightFace.

I care about systems that are secure, observable, and actually deployed — Celery queues that don't drop jobs, inference servers that survive bad RTSP frames, and encryption that holds up to audit.

AI/CV Engineer · YOLO11 · FastAPI · NVIDIA T4
